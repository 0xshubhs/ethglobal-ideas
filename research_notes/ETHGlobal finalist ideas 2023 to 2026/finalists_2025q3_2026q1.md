# ETHGlobal finalists, September 2025 to February 2026 (New Delhi 2025, ETHOnline 2025, Buenos Aires 2025, HackMoney 2026)

## Which events in this window had ETHGlobal finalists, and who were they?

### Takeaway
Four ETHGlobal hackathons fall in the window, and each named 10 "ETHGlobal Finalists" (the prizes page for each says "For the top 10 teams"): New Delhi 2025 (in person), ETHOnline 2025 (online), Buenos Aires 2025 (in person, at Devconnect) and HackMoney 2026 (online). That makes 40 finalists. 39 carry the official "ETHGlobal ... Finalist" badge on their showcase pages. The 40th, Hubble Trading Arena (Buenos Aires), is in ETHGlobal's announced top 10 as relayed by press, but its showcase page shows no badge.

### Cited Findings

**Method and verification**
- I listed every event on the ETHGlobal events page and filtered by `type: hackathon` and start date. Only four hackathons start between 2025-09-01 and 2026-02-28: `newdelhi` (2025-09-26 to 2025-09-28, physical), `ethonline2025` (2025-10-10 to 2025-10-31, virtual), `buenosaires` (2025-11-21 to 2025-11-23, physical) and `hackmoney2026` (2026-01-30 to 2026-02-11, virtual). Everything else in the window is a meetup, cafe/cowork, summit (Pragma New Delhi on 2025-09-25, Pragma Buenos Aires on 2025-11-20) or demo day, and none of those has finalists. The nearest hackathons outside the window are New York 2025 (2025-08-15 to 17) and Cannes 2026 (2026-04-03 to 05). [ETHGlobal events](https://ethglobal.com/events)
- Each event's prizes page has an "ETHGlobal <Event> Finalists" prize described as "For the top 10 teams". [New Delhi prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/newdelhi/prizes); [ETHOnline 2025 prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2025/prizes); [Buenos Aires prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/buenosaires/prizes); [HackMoney 2026 prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/hackmoney2026/prizes)
- How I found the finalists:
  1. I paged through every showcase listing page per event (`https://ethglobal.com/showcase?events=<slug>&page=N`).
  2. I flagged projects whose embedded prize objects include the ETHGlobal organization (uuid `xdat5`, the same org logo as the Finalists prize).
  3. I opened every flagged project page and confirmed the badge text "ETHGlobal <Event> 2025 Finalist" or "HackMoney 2026 Finalist".
  
  This gave 10, 10, 9 and 10. Buenos Aires was one short: Hubble Trading Arena is in the listing, but its prize array is empty and its project page shows no "Winner of" section. [Buenos Aires showcase](https://ethglobal.com/showcase?events=buenosaires); [Hubble Trading Arena](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/hubble-trading-arena-oddx1)
- ETHGlobal's FAQ on the event pages: "The ETHGlobal Finalist Track is separate and does not count as a Partner Prize." A project may apply to at most 3 partner prizes. Submissions that "rely entirely on AI without meaningful contributions from team members may not be eligible for partner prizes or finalist consideration." [ETHGlobal New Delhi](https://ethglobal.com/events/newdelhi); [ETHOnline 2025](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2025)
- Finalist perks (Finalists Pack): 1000 USDC per finalist team member and an $828 discount on ETHGlobal Plus. ETHOnline 2025, Buenos Aires and HackMoney 2026 add a $500 flight reimbursement per member. New Delhi's pack also includes a Pragma Buenos Aires ticket. [New Delhi pack](https://ethglobal.com/packs/newdelhi-finalist); [ETHOnline pack](https://ethglobal.com/packs/ethonline2025-finalist); [Buenos Aires pack](https://ethglobal.com/packs/buenosaires-finalist); [HackMoney pack](https://ethglobal.com/packs/hackmoney2026-finalist)

## What do the finalist descriptions say they built, and what did the demo prove?

### Takeaway
Most finalists describe a working end-to-end flow on a real or test chain, not just a concept. The strongest "verifiable promise" demos are physical or cryptographic:
- offline BLE transaction relay (NoNet)
- a camera whose photos verify on-chain (LensMint)
- a rented robot unlocked by x402 payment (Paybot)
- a ZK proof of an authenticator code required for every wallet transaction (ChronoVault)
- live bots trading peer-to-peer through a Uniswap v4 hook on Base mainnet (claw2claw)

Several finalists openly state that parts were mocked or simplified, and still made the top 10.

### Cited Findings
- NoNet: transactions are "signed locally, split into 6-byte fragments, and broadcast via BLE advertisements"; gateways "reassemble the transaction, verify signatures/nonces, and then submit"; "Receipts are propagated back through the mesh so the originator sees confirmations even offline." [NoNet](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/nonet-xyebu)
- LensMint: "When a photo is taken, the image is signed using the device key and its SHA-256 hash is generated", vlayer produces a ZK proof, and "A RISC Zero verifier contract validates the ZK proof entirely on-chain". People in the photo scan a QR code to claim the NFT. [LensMint Camera](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/lensmint-camera-yzu6m)
- Paybot: "The demo controls an actual UGV (Unmanned Ground Vehicle) rover accessible at a physical location", unlocked after a QUSD payment, with a facilitator paying gas. [Paybot](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/paybot-q7grd)
- ChronoVault: "every UserOperation must include a valid ZK proof corresponding to the current block timestamp", and the TOTP secret "never leaves the user's device". The project includes 35 test cases. [ChronoVault](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/chronovault-9bydw)
- claw2claw: "Several OpenClaw bots are actively trading on the deployed platform right now. P2P trades settle on-chain through the hook on Base mainnet." [claw2claw](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/claw2claw-3uxgx)
- payload.exchange shows x402 inside ChatGPT: "end users can experience this x402 resource access paid with their action from a native ChatGPT Payload Exchange app." [payload.exchange](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/payload-exchange-x07pi)
- Sippy's demo is a text message: "Users simply text 'send 5 to +573001234567' and money moves instantly on Arbitrum". It notes WhatsApp is "Limited to 5 test numbers until Meta approval for production." [Sippy](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/sippy-2smms)
- calendefi's demo is a calendar event: "Send 5 ETH to fabianferno.eth tomorrow at 2pm", with RSVP-based multisig approval. [calendefi](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/calendefi-6d3ji)
- Oikonomos: "The whole 5-phase pipeline (wallet derivation, funding, ERC-8004 registration, Nostr token launch, delegation, ENS) runs in ~22 seconds from a single API call" (punctuation adapted). [Oikonomos](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/oikonomos-w6z57)

- Finalists that admit simplifications or mocks:
  - KagamiAI "set up mock x402 payment flows to validate real-world wallet transfers" [KagamiAI](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/kagamiai-h7yq4)

### Inferences
- Judges appear to reward a single, legible, end-to-end moment the audience can watch: a message moves money, a photo verifies, a robot unlocks, a bot trades. They seem to forgive honestly disclosed shortcuts behind it.
- Many finalists stack several new standards in one flow (x402 with ERC-8004 and ENS; EIP-7702 with ERC-4337; Uniswap v4 hooks with ZK). Novel standard composition looks like a common trait of finalist builds in this window.

### Gaps
- The showcase pages do not show judges' scores or reasons. I found no ETHGlobal commentary explaining why each finalist was chosen.
- I did not watch the demo videos, so the "demo moment" column reflects what each team wrote, not what was shown live.
