# 0G at ETHGlobal: track history, winners and what its judges reward

Written 2026-10-31 for ETHGlobal Mumbai (5 to 7 Nov 2026). Fresh builds only: 0G's Continuity track ("Keep Building on 0G") is out of scope for the team and appears here only where it shows a trend.

## How this was made

- Sponsor lists for every ETHGlobal hackathon from ETHGlobal Istanbul (Nov 2023) to ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 were read from each event's `/prizes` page (the event index is at [ethglobal.com/events](https://ethglobal.com/events)). 0G appears as a sponsor at exactly **seven past events plus Mumbai**: Trifecta 2025, Cannes 2025, New Delhi 2025, Buenos Aires 2025, Cannes 2026, Open Agents 2026 and Lisbon 2026. It is absent from every 2023 and 2024 event, and from Agentic Ethereum, Taipei, Prague, Unite, New York 2025, ETHOnline 2025, HackMoney 2026, New York 2026, ETHOnline 2026 and Tokyo 2026.
- Track text comes from each `https://ethglobal.com/events/<slug>/prizes/0g` page, fetched 2026-10-31 with a plain HTTP client (no bot check on those pages).
- Winners come from the showcase listings (`https://ethglobal.com/showcase?events=<slug>&page=N`, every page read, about 2.5 s apart), where a project's card carries the logo of each organisation whose prize it won (0G's organisation id is `g8xu4`). Places come from each winner's showcase page ("Winner of" badges). 20 of 53 project pages first returned a bot check ("Enable JavaScript and cookies to continue"). I did not try to get around it; I waited and retried once at 10 s spacing, which loaded 19 of them. **Lortnoc Tahc stayed blocked**, so its details come from `research_notes/ETHGlobal finalist ideas 2023 to 2026/finalists_2026.md` and 0G's Lisbon recap.
- The Trifecta showcase filter returns no projects, so Trifecta winners come from 0G's own blog.
- Non-winners in the comparisons are projects at the same event whose showcase tagline names 0G but whose card carries no 0G prize logo. That is a lower bound on 0G usage, because many entries mention 0G only in their body text.
- **Facts carry a URL. Lines marked Judgement are my inference.**

## Summary (Judgement)

1. **0G's judges reward a 0G call that the product cannot live without, plus evidence of it.** The requirement grew from "integrate at least ONE core 0G component" (Cannes 2025) to "Could your project exist without 0G?" (Buenos Aires 2025) to request IDs, tx hashes and "One sentence on what breaks if 0G is removed" (Mumbai). The Lisbon 2026 winners were already logging request IDs, provider addresses and TEE verification without being asked; Mumbai made that habit a requirement.
2. **Composition across layers wins.** Almost every 2026 winner uses Compute plus Storage plus Chain, and 0G's Lisbon recap says so directly. Storage-only entries won only in a Wildcard track.
3. **In crowded tracks, the winners added a verifier.** At Open Agents, about 80 entries named 0G in their tagline and 10 won. The winners shipped something that checks a claim: a negative-control fake agent, on-chain attestation checks before slashing, a TEE signature recovered on another chain. The losers were mostly generic memory layers, iNFT wrappers and agent frameworks.
4. **The 0G room and the finalist room are different audiences.** Four Open Agents finalists built on 0G and won no 0G prize (LPlens, Slopstock, Clan World, Mnemosyne). At Lisbon, the two finalists that did win 0G prizes (KOLlateral, Hands Unchained) made 0G inference the thing that could not be faked.
5. **Mumbai is compute-first.** Infra/tooling, DeFi, OpenClaw and iNFT tracks are gone. All three fresh-build tracks are about consuming 0G Private Computer inference: a consumer app burning credits, a robot whose decisions are model calls, and an agent whose earnings buy its own inference. 0G's text says unused iAI credits mean "thousands go to waste daily".

## Event by event

### Trifecta, online, March 2025 ($2,500)

Source: [Trifecta 0G prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/trifecta/prizes/0g).

- **"Integrating 0G Storage"**, $1,200, "Up to 3 teams will receive $400". Verbatim requirements: "Your project must integrate 0G Storage as a core component of your dApp" and "Submissions must include a demonstration video showing real data being stored and retrieved from the 0G Storage network." Bonus: "Creative utilization of both blob storage and key-value storage features" and "Projects that demonstrate significant advantages over centralized storage alternatives".
- **"Building with 0G Compute"**, $1,300 (1st $800, 2nd $500). "This track challenges builders to create solutions that integrate large language models (LLMs) and other AI inferences with TEEML verification." Requirement: "Your project must integrate at least one LLM service available on the 0G Compute Network". Bonus: "Solutions that demonstrate verifiable and transparent AI results".
- Winners ([0G blog, 16 May 2025](https://0g.ai/blog/0g-latest-hackathon-highlights)): Storage: **CareAI** (privacy-preserving mental health assistant with ZKML), **Model Wars** (ML model marketplace where performance sets rewards), **Clampify** (fine-tuning interface for zkML models). Compute: **OGxbt** (2nd, a Bitcoin oracle using AI agents to validate cross-chain data). The blog says "none of the projects that submitted met the bar for the $800 first prize."
- Judgement: the first evidence that 0G withholds places rather than pay a weak entry. Mumbai's Physical AI track carries the same warning in writing.

### Cannes, July 2025 ($5,000)

Source: [Cannes 0G prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/cannes/prizes/0g).

- **"Most Innovative Use of 0G Ecosystem"**, $5,000, "Up to 3 teams will receive $1,666". Verbatim: "We want to see projects that couldn't exist without 0G's infrastructure." Requirements: "Must integrate at least ONE core 0G component. Integration must be functional and demonstrate real usage" and "Fully functional demo deployed on 0G testnet."
- Winners (all pool winners, no places):
  - [PrivyCycle](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/privycycle-anndr): period tracker with on-device encryption and "Anonymized AI (0G Compute)" insights for partner and doctor. Also won Privy and Zircuit, and was an ETHGlobal finalist.
  - [Warriors AI-rena](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/warriors-ai-rena-f1owp): battle arena where 0G Compute picks moves that are ECDSA-signed and checked on-chain with `ECDSA.recover()`, and NFT metadata is a 0G Storage root hash used as the tokenURI.
  - [AInfluencer](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/ainfluencer-moreg): an INFT contract on 0G testnet takes paid "feed" messages; a LLaMA-3 call on 0G Compute writes the topic and script, and the pipeline publishes a video. Also won Fluence.
- Non-winners naming 0G: [ContractObituarie](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/contractobituarie-vf879) ("uses 0G AI to identify and publish reports on dead, exploited, or deprecated smart contracts"); [Braivo](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/braivo-tnjw5) (image upload to 0G Storage with Merkle proofs); Solidity Cell (escape-room game "using... 0G AI").
- Judgement: the winners put 0G inside a loop that changes state (a signed move verified on-chain, a paid message that triggers generation). The losers made one call (a risk label) or used one layer (storage upload). ContractObituarie is the closest thing to a security product, and it lost with a thin, one-shot AI call.

The likely difference is a demo of something live on mainnet (WEAVE's EchoRepeater contract, Whale.fun's battles) versus a library. This event is the outlier in 0G's history and the weakest guide to Mumbai.

### Cannes 2026, April 2026 ($15,000)

Source: [Cannes 2026 0G prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/cannes2026/prizes/0g).

- **"Best OpenClaw Agent on 0G"**, $6,000 (3,000 / 2,000 / 1,000): "Build the best applications or extensions that integrate OpenClaw (the viral open-source AI agent framework) or alternative Claw agents (ZeroClaw, NullClaw, etc) with 0G's decentralized infrastructure. Use 0G Compute, Storage for persistent memory, Chain for on-chain actions/payments, and iNFTs (ERC-7857) for agent ownership/composability."
- **"Best DeFi App on 0G"**, $6,000 (3,000 / 2,000 / 1,000): "We're looking for AI-native DeFi experiences that are autonomous, verifiable, and economically self-sustaining." Ideas included "Multi-agent DeFi swarm" and "AI-powered prediction market with on-chain model provenance + verifiable/Sealed Inference".
- **"Wildcard on 0G"**, $3,000 (2 x $1,500): "Build anything creative that showcases the full power of 0G's AI-native L1 but doesn't fit neatly into OpenClaw or DeFi categories."
- Winners:
  - OpenClaw: 1st [Croisette.cc](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/croisette-cc-8vdfk) (24/7 investing by ZeroClaw agents; "every agent's core files are hashed into a manifest committed to 0G Storage", re-hashed at runtime to flag prompt-injection tampering). 2nd [DIVE](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/dive-5hxbp) (AI-swarm oracle for prediction markets with OpenClaw nodes as ERC-7857 iNFTs, "inference runs within 0G Compute TEEs", and evidence on 0G Storage; also won World 1st and Hedera, and was a finalist). 3rd [Orchestra](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/orchestra-mkifq) (chat-to-swap assistant; a Gatekeeper reads spending limits from 0G Storage and sends risky transactions to Ledger approval; users "toggle between centralized (Groq) and decentralized LLM inference").
  - DeFi: 1st [Shawarma Orchestrate](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/shawarma-orchestrate-rfyhe) (LangGraph swarm on 0G Compute through the broker, with `processResponse(provider, chatID, usage)` called on every completion and swarm results uploaded to 0G Storage). 2nd [Alpha Dawg](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/alpha-dawg-fh6vm) (an agent hires 10 specialist agents for $0.001 nanopayments and runs a debate in sealed inference; "Arc = money, 0G = brain, Hedera = truth"; also won Hedera 2nd). 3rd [Don't Get Drained](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/don-t-get-drained-n4bdy) (a marketplace of AI guard agents that review a Safe transaction against the user's plain-English intent; rootHash proofs go to an `InferenceGuard` Safe Guard).
  - Wildcard: [CaaS](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/caas-ivt4s) (World-ID-gated "Claws" with encrypted memory on 0G Storage and ERC-7857 identity, paid in WLD; also won World 3rd) and [Indelible](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/indelible-6b4zf) (archives political statements as Merkle-verified blobs on 0G Storage).
- Non-winners naming 0G: [Underlay](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/underlay-ikbjb) (multi-leg Polymarket positions priced by a 0G LLM, reasoning pinned to 0G Storage), [Radegast](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/radegast-jnu3c) (agents trade tokenized stocks on 0G Chain), Zuza (chat DeFi "Powered by 0G + Uniswap"), 0G-Gang (context NFTs), soulvault (swarm continuity layer).
- Judgement: security wins here when the model's verdict is enforced, not just shown: Don't Get Drained gates a Safe and Croisette re-hashes agent files. Underlay is as deep as Shawarma but sits in a crowded category (prediction markets) and lists 0G as one of five layers. The rule I take from this: **0G has to be the decision, not one input to a decision made elsewhere.** Orchestra's Groq toggle is a counter-example (0G was optional and it still placed 3rd), which suggests the bar was lower at a three-track event with smaller pools per track.

Source: [Open Agents 0G prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/openagents/prizes/0g).

- **"Best Agent Framework, Tooling & Core Extensions"**, $7,500 (2,500 / 2,000 / 1,500 / 1,000 / 500): "This track is strictly for framework-level work." Must include "At least one working example agent built using your framework/tooling".
- **"Best Autonomous Agents, Swarms & iNFT Innovations"**, $7,500 ("Up to 5 teams will receive $1,500"): "This track is strictly for building the actual agents." Must include "For swarms: clear explanation of how agents communicate and coordinate" and "For iNFT projects: link to the minted iNFT on 0G explorer + proof that the intelligence/memory is embedded".
- Winners, framework track:
  1. [ACL](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/acl-6hwos): turns "the brand-new ERC-8183 (Agentic Commerce Protocol) from a bare on-chain escrow into a working, end-to-end commerce pipeline deployed on 0G Galileo"; nine `@acl/*` packages and evaluation through TEE-attested 0G Compute. Also won ENS 1st.
  2. [Mendel](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/mendel-319b2): breeds trading-strategy genomes as ERC-7857 agents; an LLM gatekeeper on 0G Compute whose TEE attestation is checked on every cycle.
  3. [Immunity](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/immunity-eg56a) ([Open Agents page](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/immunity-urob5)): a three-tier transaction check for agents (AXL gossip cache, an on-chain registry on 0G, then TEE LLM evaluation on 0G Compute). It also published "standardized SKILL for 0G stack ... with many gotchas".
  4. [cortex](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/cortex-cactx): agent brains encrypted on 0G Storage and KV, "the runtime fails closed if TeeML verification does not pass", ENS discovery and AXL messaging.
  5. [FFE](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/ffe-nmr2w): multi-party fine-tuning; contributors encrypt to a 0G Tapp enclave key whose attestation the SDK checks, and the coordinator contract "verifies a TEE-signed rejection certificate before slashing".
- Winners, agents and iNFT pool: [Federated Reserve](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/federated-reserve-n10yp) (50 state-treasurer agents; ECIES-sealed keys mean "transferring the iNFT transfers the underlying intelligence"), [Brainpedia](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/brainpedia-ctx9g) (Obsidian vault becomes a paid-per-query iNFT brain; the answer is released only after the payment receipt is decoded on-chain; also won ENS 2nd), [Mnemo Agents](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/mnemo-agents-js7hy) (breedable iNFT agents with royalties up the lineage), [AgentMeshx](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/agentmeshx-jq8hz) (four specialist audit agents on an AXL mesh; an `AuditAttestation` contract on 0G records report hash, storage root and counts), [CodeGuardian iNFT](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/codeguardian-inft-0u905) (a "Proof-of-Intelligence" verifier with "FakeAgent ... as a negative control to show the verifier rejects metadata-only agents").
- Non-winners: the showcase lists about 80 taglines that name 0G against 10 winners. Typical losers were memory layers and frameworks: [0G Forge](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/0g-forge-wmv0f), Hiveclaw, ZeroMem, 0G-CLAW, 0MCP, Tessra; iNFT marketplaces (OpenDock, The Yard, AgentFund); and audit agents (AI Auditor, JURI Protocol, RAXC). Four ETHGlobal finalists built on 0G and won no 0G prize: [LPlens](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/lplens-7sijq), Slopstock, Clan World and Mnemosyne (`finalists_2026.md`). [era](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/era-a1dv9) (three iNFT personas with sealed receipts and 20+ on-chain Invocation events) also won nothing.
- Judgement: in the most crowded 0G field on record, the winners had either (a) a standard that had just shipped (ERC-8183 in ACL), (b) a mechanism that verifies 0G output and acts on it (FFE slashing, cortex failing closed, CodeGuardian's negative control, Brainpedia's pay-to-read check), or (c) a large, legible simulation (Federated Reserve's 50 agents). LPlens lost the 0G prize while winning a finalist slot. Its heavy lifting is a 1,000-swap replay through Uniswap math; 0G only writes the closing verdict, so removing 0G would leave the core intact. That is exactly what Mumbai's "Apps where the AI call could be removed without anyone noticing" now rules out.

Source: [Lisbon 2026 0G prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/lisbon2026/prizes/0g); recap: [0G blog, 17 Aug 2026](https://0g.ai/blog/ethglobal-lisbon-2026-recap).

- **"Best Infrastructure & Tooling on 0G"**, $4,500 (3 x $1,500): "The builder layer." Ideas included "Verification, guardrail, or auditor layer for on-chain agent actions".
- Winners, AI Product:
  1. [0run](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/0run-420af): AI running coaches as ERC-7857-style iNFTs; GPX runs encrypted on 0G Storage with a wallet-derived key; "effort score TEE-attested via processResponse, every response carries x_0g_trace as proof of use"; email login through Privy; the reference agent-NFT "was undeployable (24.6KB, EIP-170)", so the team wrote its own.   3. [KOLlateral](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/kollateral-ho1d1): influencer calls priced against DEX history; "that inference is the part we cannot fake", sent "with `verify_tee` turned on and a private trust-mode header". Also won Uniswap API 1st and was a finalist.

- Recap facts ([0G blog](https://0g.ai/blog/ethglobal-lisbon-2026-recap)): 67 projects in 0G tracks, 49 demoed live, "57 projects (85%) with repo-verified meaningful 0G integration", 52 from scratch and 15 continuing. Component use: Compute 82%, Storage 67%, Agentic ID 46%, Chain about 43%. Judging "combined live demos with repo-level review" by people who "read source code the way an auditor would"; "teams under-reported their own integrations"; "Teams composed multiple 0G layers into single products."

- Judgement: two of the three Infrastructure winners are security guards, and Do Not Rug Me (arguably deeper on 0G signature verification) lost to them. The most plausible reasons are presentation and track fit: AEGIS and Superpose each had one sentence a judge could repeat, while Do Not Rug Me's page leads with a long list of chain quirks. Namesake and Sluice show that **a single base-URL swap does not place**. The AI Product podium shows that 0G judges pay most for consumer products where the 0G output is the scored, paid or graded thing (a coach's effort score, a grade that releases pay, a call that becomes a public record).

## Why winners beat other entries (Judgement, with examples)

| What 0G judges reward | Winning examples | Losing examples at the same event | How Mumbai encodes it |

## Sourced technical facts that shape the ideas

- **Router**: OpenAI-compatible at `https://router-api.0g.ai/v1`. Testnet is `https://router-api-testnet.integratenetwork.work/v1`, with "separate API keys and balances" ([Router overview](https://docs.0g.ai/developer-hub/building-on-0g/compute-network/router/overview)).
- **Request ID**: "Every Router response carries an `x_0g_trace` object" with `request_id`, `provider` (on-chain address) and `billing.input_cost / output_cost / total_cost` "in neuron"; `tee_verified` is present "only when `verify_tee: true` was set" ([Chat Completions](https://docs.0g.ai/developer-hub/building-on-0g/compute-network/router/features/chat-completions)).
- **Trust tiers**: `private` routes only to TeeML providers ("Sealed inference: prompts never leave the enclave"); `verified` adds TeeTLS; `standard` is "Non-verifiable: no attestation or signed proof to check" (Claude and GPT-5.6 families are served there). Set the tier with the header `X-0G-Provider-Trust-Mode: private` or per key; when no TeeML provider is available the request gets a 503 `no_provider_for_trust_mode` and "never silently falls back to a lower tier" ([Privacy & ZDR](https://docs.0g.ai/developer-hub/building-on-0g/compute-network/router/privacy)).
- **Models today** (public `GET /v1/models`, 119 entries, read 2026-10-31): only 9 are TeeML: `0gm-1.0-35b-a3b`, `0gm-1.0-35b-a3b-sia`, `glm-5.3`, `whisper-large-v3`, `z-image-turbo`, `qwen3-embedding-0.6b`, `qwen3-embedding-8b`, and two "decisions" models, `laya` and `vela-2.0-9b`. These answer "noul / choice / score questions with calibrated probabilities instead of text" through `POST /decisions`. Vision and omni models (`qwen3-vl-plus`, `qwen3-omni-flash`), `seedance`, `minimax-h3` and the GLM-5.2 family are TeeTLS (verified, not sealed); `seedream` and `hy-image` carry no verifiability ([models endpoint](https://router-api.0g.ai/v1/models)).
- **Keys and balance**: `sk-` keys call inference; `mk-` keys with `account:read` read `/v1/account/balance` and `/v1/account/usage/history`. "No OAuth flow, no wallet signature per request" ([Authentication](https://docs.0g.ai/developer-hub/building-on-0g/compute-network/router/authentication)). An empty balance returns `402 insufficient_balance`. Deposits go to the 0G Payment Layer (mainnet `0xA3b15Bd2aD18BFB6b5f92D8AA9F444Dd59d1cE32`, testnet `0x0AD9690e0b34aB2d493DE02cDF149ee34f6C9939`), and "The deposit belongs to your wallet address" ([Deposits & Billing](https://docs.0g.ai/developer-hub/building-on-0g/compute-network/router/account/deposits)).
- **Price caps per request**: `X-0G-Provider-Max-Price-Usd-Prompt` and `-Completion` "Drops every provider above the ceiling before sorting and failover" ([Routing](https://docs.0g.ai/developer-hub/building-on-0g/compute-network/router/routing)).
- **comfy**: runs on 0G mainnet (chain 16661) with real 0G. "A token's compute share is the part of its creator fees paid to the pool's compute before the creator and their recipients divide the rest. Its credit lands in the 0G Payment Layer account of the creator wallet". `comfy compute key mint --agent 42 --limit-og 2` makes a capped `sk-` key with "a limit a day", which only a local proxy holds; the proxy "refuses one that what is left of the key's limit for today does not cover". Signing commands must be run "in your own terminal, never through an agent" ([comfy-cli 0.9.4 README on npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/comfy-cli), published 29 Oct 2026; [comfy docs](https://www.comfy.fun/docs)).
- **zerobot**: contracts `RobotRegistry` (identity, roles, "command pricing", `setPublicCommands`) and `RobotCommandDispatcher` ("Command queue with monotonic nonces, 5-minute expiry, payment enforcement, execution receipts") on Galileo. `OperatorNode` "rejects commands outside `allowedApiIds`" and "treats 5s of silence or a closed channel as a lost link". Receipts "land 4-5s after each move". `AIBroker.resolvePrompt` turns prompts into command sequences via 0G Compute. A Go2 "Move command is maintained for 1 second" ([AGENTS.md](https://github.com/0gfoundation/zerobot/blob/main/AGENTS.md); [README](https://github.com/0gfoundation/zerobot)).
- **iAI**: "Each staked iAI activates more than $1 of daily AI compute credits, 1.271 credits a day"; "iAI is not available in some jurisdictions" ([iAI docs](https://iai.finance/docs)). How a third-party app signs a user in with their 0G account is not documented in any page I could read; see the workshop questions in the ideas file.
- **Gotchas reported by past winners** (from their showcase pages): Galileo chain id 16602 ([Rerun](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/rerun-q5ypt)); `@0gfoundation/0g-ts-sdk` versus `@0glabs` naming cost a team an hour, and ethers v6 needs a dummy ENS plugin on 0G ([Alpha Dawg](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/alpha-dawg-fh6vm)); the storage SDK uses `XMLHttpRequest` and breaks in Node servers ([Underlay](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/underlay-ikbjb)); 0G has no RIP-7212, EntryPoint v0.8 has no code (v0.7 only), and receipts lag ([Do Not Rug Me](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/do-not-rug-me-7i15x)); the reference agent-NFT exceeds EIP-170 ([0run](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/0run-420af)); broker request headers are single-use ([Alpha Dawg](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/alpha-dawg-fh6vm)).
