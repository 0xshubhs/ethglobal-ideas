# Hedera at ETHGlobal: past tracks, winners and what its judges reward

Dossier for ETHGlobal Mumbai (5 to 7 Nov 2026). Researched 2026-10-31. Companion idea file: `track_ideas/ideas_draft/hedera.md`.

## H0. Summary

- **Fact.** Hedera offered prizes at 14 ETHGlobal hackathons between August 2024 and September 2026, from ETHOnline 2024 to ETHOnline 2026, and Mumbai is the 15th. It paid $10,000 per event through Buenos Aires 2025 and $15,000 per event from Cannes 2026 on. It did not sponsor any ETHGlobal hackathon from New York 2023 to Brussels 2024, nor Agentic Ethereum, Trifecta, Taipei, Unite DeFi, HackMoney 2026, Open Agents or Tokyo 2026. ([ETHGlobal events](https://ethglobal.com/events); each event's `/prizes` page, listed in H2)
- **Fact.** The showcase data shows 94 Hedera prize awards to 92 projects across those 14 events, and every one of those project pages was opened (H4). ([showcase](https://ethglobal.com/showcase))
- **Judgement.** The problem statements moved through four eras, each one narrower and closer to finance than the last: "build anything" starter bounties (2024); AI plus EVM tracks with an Overall prize and a published seven-part rubric (2025); Agent Kit, A2A and plugin tracks (late 2025); and, from 2026, a stable three-track menu: "AI & Agentic Payments", "Tokenization" and "No Solidity Allowed", plus one rotating partner or tooling track. Mumbai keeps the same three tracks and tightens each one: x402 is now mandatory (not one option among five), the tokenization track now requires Hedera's open-source studios (not just HTS), and "No Solidity" is unchanged apart from fewer awards.
- **Judgement, why winners won.** In 2026 the winners differ from the Hedera-using projects that lost in three ways. First, several native services each do a distinct job. Second, at least one primitive is used in a way a judge would not predict: an HCS topic as an order book, a contract that holds its own HTS KYC key, an HIP-336 allowance for agent spending, a scheduled call that closes a loan. Third, they make a scoped, honest claim backed by real data or devices. Spending guards for agents, the most common idea at the booth, mostly lost.
- **Fact.** Hedera's own published hackathon rubric weights Execution 20%, Success (Hedera accounts and TPS) 20%, Integration 15%, Validation (market feedback) 15%, Innovation 10%, Feasibility 10% and Pitch 10%, and pays the top Integration score only when "A Hedera service is integrated in a way that was not obvious". The same rubric, in a shorter form, was printed on the Prague 2025 prize page. ([hedera-skills judging criteria](https://github.com/hedera-dev/hedera-skills/blob/master/plugins/hackathon-helper/skills/validate-submission/references/judging-criteria.md); [Prague 2025 Hedera prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/prague/prizes/hedera); H6)

## H1. Method and sources

- Event list: every event of type "hackathon" from 2023-09 onward was parsed from the embedded data of [ethglobal.com/events](https://ethglobal.com/events) (35 hackathons, New York 2023 to Mumbai).
- Tracks: each event's [prizes page](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2024/prizes) was fetched once, slowly, and the sponsor object with slug `hedera` was decoded, including description, qualifications and prize items. Where a description was stored as a separate data chunk, it was resolved from the page's flight data.
- Winners: the showcase listing (`https://ethglobal.com/showcase?events=<slug>&page=N`) was paged to the end for each of the 14 events (6,397 projects in total), and every project whose prize list includes the Hedera organization (uuid `bdi3h`) was opened. Each project page names the prize ("Winner of" badge data), and the prize item's uuid was matched to the event's prize list to recover the track and place.
- Non-winning comparison set: projects at the same event whose name, tagline or ETHGlobal's auto-generated summary mention Hedera, HBAR, HTS, HCS, Hiero or HashScan, but which carry no Hedera prize. **Caveat:** a mention does not prove the team selected Hedera's prize, so "non-winner" means "used or mentioned Hedera and did not win a Hedera prize". The listing also carries ETHGlobal's own automated `autoOriginality`, `autoPracticality` and `autoTechnicality` scores (1 to 10) for 2026 events. ETHGlobal does not document how they are made, so they are used only as a rough cross-check.
- Access: no bot check appeared. Some requests returned HTTP 429 (other research agents shared the network), and those were retried after a pause; every winner page was eventually fetched. The Hedera blog has no posts announcing ETHGlobal winners (search, 2026-10-31), so the showcase is the only winner source.
- Claims about what a project built are the team's own showcase text; nobody here watched the demo videos.

## H3. Problem statements by era (verbatim where possible)

### Era 1, 2024: starter bounties and "build anything" (ETHOnline 2024 to Bangkok)

- Ecosystem Builder Bounty: "Build something that is new to the Hedera ecosystem, or brings value to the Hedera ecosystem." Qualifications: "Must be deployed on (or send transactions to) the Hedera Testnet.", "Must use at least two of the following: HTS, HCS, or HSCS.", "The top 3 submissions will be judged and awarded based on the judging rubric." ([ETHOnline 2024](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2024/prizes/hedera))
- EVM Starter Bounty: "Build anything you like", "Submissions that use multiple Hedera services will be prioritized.", "Otherwise, submissions are prioritized based on the order of submission." Must use HSCS. The Native Services Starter Bounty is the same with "at least one of Hedera Token Service (HTS) or Hedera Consensus Service (HCS)". (same source; repeated at [Singapore](https://ethglobal.com/events/singapore2024/prizes/hedera), [San Francisco](https://ethglobal.com/events/sanfrancisco2024/prizes/hedera), [Bangkok](https://ethglobal.com/events/bangkok/prizes/hedera))
- **Judgement.** These were onboarding bounties paid partly on submission order, not quality. They tell us little about taste, except that "multiple Hedera services" has been the tie-breaker since the first event.

### Era 2, 2025: AI and EVM tracks plus an Overall prize, with a published rubric (Prague to New Delhi)

- "AI, Agents & Hedera Services" (Prague) and "AI on Hedera" (Cannes, New York, New Delhi): "Build innovative applications, agents, tooling, or infrastructure that combine AI/ML (including LLMs and autonomous agents) with Hedera network services. The focus is on demonstrating novel AI-blockchain integrations that create real utility for developers or end-users." Later qualifications: "Utilise at least one Hedera service: Smart Contracts (EVM), Token Service, Consensus Service, Scheduled Transactions, or Mirror Node.", "Incorporate AI in a material way e.g. Hedera Agent Kit SDK, Hedera Eliza Plugin, Federated Learning, AI Data Marketplaces.", "Verify any smart contracts on Hashscan", "Include a demo video (≤ 5 minutes)". ([Cannes 2025](https://ethglobal.com/events/cannes/prizes/hedera); [New York 2025](https://ethglobal.com/events/newyork2025/prizes/hedera))
- "Hedera EVM: Smart Contracts & Cross-Chain Integrations": "Build and deploy innovative Smart Contract based applications on Hedera. The focus is on leveraging Hedera's EVM compatibility and integration into the Ethereum ecosystem." Extra points: oracles (Chainlink, Pyth, Supra), bridges (LayerZero, HashPort, Chainlink CCIP), "HTS System Contracts for a hybrid EVM and Native Service application", native Hedera wallets. (same sources)
- Overall prize: "The Overall Winner is the best project out of both AI and EVM tracks." (same sources)
- Prague's qualifications printed the rubric: "Judging Criteria: Innovation (originality of the idea), Feasibility (technical viability), Execution (completeness & code quality), Integration (depth of Hedera usage), Validation (market/user potential), Impact (contribution to Hedera metrics (accounts, TPS, etc.)), Pitch (clarity and persuasiveness of presentation)". ([Prague 2025](https://ethglobal.com/events/prague/prizes/hedera))

### Era 3, late 2025: Agent Kit, A2A, plugins and an "EVM Innovator" track (ETHOnline 2025, Buenos Aires)

- "Best Use of Hedera Agent Kit & Google A2A": "Create one or more agents using the Hedera Agent Kit that can send and receive A2A messages and, if appropriate, settle payments via AP2 using Hedera tokens." Requirements: "Multi-agent communication", "Agent Kit integration", "Bonus points for using multiple Hedera services or advanced features like human-in-the-loop modes." ([ETHOnline 2025](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2025/prizes/hedera); [Buenos Aires](https://ethglobal.com/events/buenosaires/prizes/hedera))
- "Best Plug-In for Hedera Agent Kit": "rewards the most useful and well-architected new plugin that introduces new capabilities"; it must be "installable via npm or similar". (same sources)
- "Best Hedera x Lit Protocol Vincent Ability" (ETHOnline 2025 only): a Vincent "ability" using Hedera services, showing "how a user delegates permissions and the skill executes actions". ([ETHOnline 2025](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2025/prizes/hedera))
- "EVM Innovator Track": "using cross-chain and real-world data to build innovative dApps"; must "Integrate oracles and/or bridges". (same sources)

### Era 4, 2026: payments, tokenization and "No Solidity" (Cannes 2026 to ETHOnline 2026)

- "AI & Agentic Payments on Hedera" (Cannes, New York, Lisbon): "Build AI agents that move value autonomously on Hedera." Use "the Hedera Agent Kit, OpenClaw's Agent Commerce Protocol, the x402 payment standard, or any combination", and "the best projects will demonstrate real payment flows between agents or between agents and services." Qualification: "Build an AI agent or multi-agent system that executes at least one payment, token transfer, or financial operation on Hedera Testnet." Extra points included x402, ERC-8004 or HCS-14 identity, UCP discovery, HTS custom fees, Scheduled Transactions, HCS audit trails and the Hedera CLI. ([Cannes 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/cannes2026/prizes/hedera); [New York 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/newyork2026/prizes/hedera); [Lisbon 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/lisbon2026/prizes/hedera))
- ETHOnline 2026 narrowed it to x402: "x402 on Hedera is still short of one thing: actual services you can pay for. That is the challenge. Stand up a real x402-gated service on Hedera and build the platform that consumes it." Qualification: "Host a live x402-gated service on Hedera testnet or mainnet, settled through the Blocky402 facilitator." Extra points: "Pay-per-call inference, data, or compute metering rather than a flat per-request charge". ([ETHOnline 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes/hedera))
- "Tokenization on Hedera" (Cannes, New York, Lisbon): "Build a tokenization application using the Hedera Token Service (HTS)... This bounty is focused on real-world asset tokenization... Projects that demonstrate a clear path from physical asset to on-chain token with meaningful compliance or lifecycle management will be favoured." New York's ideas: tokenized fund shares, invoice factoring, carbon credit registry. ([Cannes 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/cannes2026/prizes/hedera); [New York 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/newyork2026/prizes/hedera))
- ETHOnline 2026 switched it to ATS: "Tokenization of Anything": "Institutional adoption is the dominant narrative in the market right now, and tokenised collateral is the sharpest edge of it... Build an enterprise finance application on Hedera using the Asset Tokenization Studio... Real asset classes and real lifecycle management will be favoured over a token with a name on it." Extra points: "A secondary market for ATS-issued assets, which the Studio does not have today" and "Contributions back upstream to ATS". ([ETHOnline 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes/hedera))
- "No Solidity Allowed" (Cannes, New York, Lisbon): "Build a working application on Hedera using only the Hedera SDK. No Solidity, no smart contracts... This bounty is designed for developers who are new to Hedera or new to blockchain." Qualifications: "Use the Hedera JavaScript/TypeScript SDK (@hashgraph/sdk) or Python SDK. No Solidity smart contracts.", "Incorporate at least two native Hedera services". Extra points: "Coherent end-to-end user experience (not just API calls)", Mirror Node, "Creative use of Hedera Consensus Service", Agent Kit ("counts as SDK usage"), "Thoughtful security approach (key management, access controls)". Ideas: timestamped proofs, token-gated access, scheduled payment bot, supply chain tracker, verifiable messaging. ([Lisbon 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/lisbon2026/prizes/hedera))
- Rotating fourth track: "ioBuilders Naryo Builder Challenge" (Cannes 2026: "Leverage Naryo to capture and filter events from at least one DLT"); "Autonomous On-Chain Automation Platform" with the Schedule Service (New York 2026 Continuity, "no bots, keepers, or off-chain cron infrastructure"); "Cross-Chain Automation Hub" with Axelar GMP (Lisbon 2026, "fully on-chain, zero off-chain relayers or keepers"); "Open Source: Improve the Hedera Harness" (ETHOnline 2026: "This track rewards contribution over greenfield"). (sources per event as above)

## H5. Why the winners won: comparison with non-winning Hedera projects

The comparison set is the 2026 projects at the same events that mention Hedera but won no Hedera prize: 13 at New York, 30 at Lisbon, 27 at Cannes and 179 at ETHOnline 2026, against 10 or 11 winners each (H1 caveat applies). ETHGlobal's automated scores are only slightly higher for winners (per-event means: Technicality 7.9 to 8.7 for winners against 7.2 to 8.3 for the rest; Originality and Practicality differ by about 0.5 or less), so the auto-scores do not explain the choice; the judges are looking at something else.

1. **Distinct jobs for several native services, described precisely.** Winners name what each service does: Dead Men Tell Tales ("The Schedule Service posts the release authorization at the deadline, the Consensus Service records an ordered audit log... the File Service plus HCS chunking stores ciphertexts"), ProvenanceChain (HCS for the fingerprint, HTS for the certificate, HFS "no admin key, meaning it cannot be deleted or modified"), Folio ("4+ native Hedera services"). Non-winners at the same events mostly "log to HCS": SentinelAI (NY 2026: "records approval decisions to Hedera Consensus Service"), Satoshi's Parabox (NY 2026: "logs audit events to Hedera Consensus Service"), VendorRank AI and LeaseLedger (Cannes 2026: "Hedera audit logging", "Hedera-anchored audit timeline"), Verdikt (Lisbon 2026: "anchors results on Hedera"). **Judgement:** an HCS audit log is now the default and no longer scores by itself; the rubric's validator literally scores "Mirror node queries only = 1 point" ([SKILL.md](https://github.com/hedera-dev/hedera-skills/blob/master/plugins/hackathon-helper/skills/validate-submission/SKILL.md)).
2. **A native primitive used in a way a judge would not predict.** Pastel turned an HCS topic into an order book with bids "ordered by Hedera consensus time" and "the declared bidder must match the transaction payer"; Wafer's contract holds its own HTS KYC and freeze keys ("no off-chain signer"); AutoVoyage used a HIP-336 allowance and found that "Hedera requires the spender... to own the transaction ID for an allowance-debited transfer", which was "undocumented for HBAR"; Rialto closed loans with a HIP-1215 scheduled call; F2F wrapped an HCS submit inside a schedule so consensus executes it on the second signature; Finvoice replayed HCS topics as its database. This is the rubric's Integration 5: "integrated in a way that was not obvious".
3. **Spending guards mostly lost.** At Lisbon 2026, Planbound ("Hedera-enforced spending accounts", auto-Technicality 9), AEGIS (policy multisig for agents), Yareon (policy-constrained procurement) and Pixport (policy-gated payouts) did not win; Ognef (default-deny mandates) did, in "No Solidity". At Cannes 2026, Bound ("containment certificates") and AegisPay did not win. At ETHOnline 2026 the winning payment entries were services people pay for (research, analysis, flight data), matching that track's text: "x402 on Hedera is still short of one thing: actual services you can pay for." **Judgement:** Hedera's judges reward the service and the market more than the guard; a guard has to be the enforcement mechanism of a real paid flow.
4. **Real data, real devices, measured numbers and stated limits.** ZoneProof served 434,000 Wake County parcels; Azimuth received real NOAA and Meteor satellites; Vouch used three physical iPhones with App Attest; Facture measured "7 to 9 HBAR an issuance"; Carpool's Python script "re-checks 60 of our on-chain claims"; NameGate reported "194 tests total, 49 of them running live"; AutoVoyage wrote "The honest hack: flight data... a curated dummy dataset". This matches the finalist-level pattern in `reports/ETHGlobal finalist ideas 2023 to 2026.md`.
5. **Ecosystem leverage and contributions.** Opto integrated Hedera DeFi (Stader HBARX, Bonzo Finance); SaucerHedge targeted SaucerSwap LPs; Dark Matter Markets built on the HCS DAO-voting convention; Aivy Quorum shipped a helper package and an Agent Kit PR (#1088) during the event; Hanvil and Scenario Engine improved Hedera's own tooling; NameGate plugged into ATS's own `IExternalControlList` "through its factory, not a fork". The rubric's Integration 5 asks for "ecosystem partners", and the validator looks for "SaucerSwap, HashPack, etc.".
6. **Compliance with the exact letter of the track.** ETHOnline 2026 required Blocky402 settlement; all three payment winners name it or a self-hosted facilitator. Tokenization winners all show a lifecycle operation (coupons, redemption, freeze, deposits). "No Solidity" winners state "no Solidity" in their text.
7. **Stacking does not hurt, but rarely pays twice.** Six 2026 Hedera winners also won World, Arc, 0G, Ledger, Sui, ENS or The Graph prizes (DIVE, MARS, Azimuth, Turnstile, Dead Men Tell Tales, AlphaDawg); the other 34 won Hedera alone, including several that integrated other sponsors (Pastel, MilFi, Vouch, Nook). **Judgement:** Hedera judges do not penalize multi-chain builds as long as Hedera has a clear, named job ("Arc = money, 0G = brain, Hedera = truth", AlphaDawg).
8. **Quality bar has risen.** In 2025, HackerPayoutsBuddy won EVM 2nd with a "How it's made" that repeats one sentence eight times, and AIR won "AI on Hedera" with an n8n chatbot "deployed on Hedera and Flow". Nothing that thin won in 2026. **Judgement:** treat 2025 winners as weak evidence of current taste.

## H6. Hedera's own judging rubric

**Fact.** The `hackathon-helper` plugin in `hedera-dev/hedera-skills`, which the Mumbai workshop "Hedera X Claude Code - AI Skills for Hackathon Builders" installs, ships "the official judging criteria" ([repo](https://github.com/hedera-dev/hedera-skills); [Mumbai event page](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai)):

| Section | Weight | What scores 5 (verbatim, condensed) |
|---|---|---|
| Innovation | 10% | "Solution that is previously unseen, on Hedera, or cross-chain"; score 1 is "essentially copies an existing Hedera platform (e.g. NFT marketplace, DEX)" |
| Feasibility | 10% | "could not be created using Web2 solutions"; "A complete Lean/Business Model Canvas was presented" |
| Execution | 20% | "fully functional solution with frictionless UI/UX"; a GTM strategy and "one or more market feedback cycles" |
| Integration | 15% | "integrates with ecosystem partners"; "A Hedera service is integrated in a way that was not obvious" |
| Validation | 15% | "Multiple market feedback cycles"; early adopters; "Some initial partnerships formed" |
| Success | 20% | "A significant increase in the number of accounts created, and the number of monthly active accounts. TPS is significantly impacted." |
| Pitch | 10% | "numbers, or metrics, presented made complete sense and were based on cited data sources"; "Hedera was a core component of the pitch" |

The validator skill adds: "Mirror node queries only = 1 point", "Multiple services + ecosystem partners + creative usage = 5 points", and it searches the repo for "Lean Canvas, monetization strategy", "a roadmap or post-hackathon plan" and "Is there a pitch deck or demo script in the repo?" ([SKILL.md](https://github.com/hedera-dev/hedera-skills/blob/master/plugins/hackathon-helper/skills/validate-submission/SKILL.md)).

**Judgement.** Partner prizes at Mumbai are judged "only from the repo, README and video". The rubric therefore rewards a README that carries a short Lean Canvas, a named user and a feedback cycle, a projected count of Hedera accounts and transactions, a list of the native services and what each does, and the ecosystem partners used. Most teams leave the 35% in Validation and Success blank.

## H9. Lessons for Mumbai (Judgement)

1. Pick a Hedera primitive and use it in a way the booth has not seen; say in one sentence what each native service does.
2. In the x402 track, sell a service people want and put the enforcement or verification in the payment path. Plain wrappers and off-chain guards are the losing majority.
3. In tokenization, use ATS or Stablecoin Studio for real lifecycle operations and aim at the gaps Hedera named: secondary markets, collateral and contributions back. Invoices are crowded (cashmeifyoucan, Finvoice, Facture, CreditMesh).
4. In "No Solidity", use three or more native services, including Scheduled Transactions or key structures, and make the security approach visible.
5. Write the README for Hedera's rubric: Lean Canvas, users, feedback cycle, projected accounts and TPS, ecosystem partners, measured numbers, limits.
6. Run the `validate-submission` skill from `hedera-dev/hedera-skills` on the repo before submitting; it encodes the rubric the judges use.
